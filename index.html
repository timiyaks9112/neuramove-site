The working day

A better record starts with what actually happened.

NeuraMove helps you connect the hours you worked with the earnings and costs you recorded. No spreadsheet to rebuild at the end of the week.

01

Record the shift.

Log your work time and earnings, and label the platform you drove for. You can add a manual entry when a record is missing.

02

Keep the costs in sight.

Track work mileage and expenses as you go, including receipt photos where useful.

03

Review the whole picture.

See trends and estimated hourly figures based on the records you have entered and tracked.

Made for the in-between

The money you made is only part of the day.

A payout alone cannot show the time and vehicle costs behind it. Bring your logged earnings, mileage, and expenses into one view to understand your own work patterns.

NeuraMove’s insights depend on the records available. There is no live platform account connection in the current beta.

Why we’re building it

Gig work is flexible. Keeping track of it should feel manageable.

NeuraMove began with a driver’s need for clearer records. We’re testing the app with a small group now, listening to what works on the road, and improving the parts that do not.

Good to know

A few straightforward answers.

Is NeuraMove free?

The current beta is free. Paid features have not launched.

Does it connect to my gig accounts?

There is no live platform account connection yet. You can log shifts manually and use supported file imports; review imported records for accuracy.

Can I use it to file my taxes?

NeuraMove provides summaries and estimates based on your records. Keep supporting receipts and mileage logs, and verify tax treatment with a qualified professional before filing.

What about Android?

The current beta is on iPhone. Android availability is still being worked on.
