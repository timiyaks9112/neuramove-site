Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about NeuraMove

Is NeuraMove free?

The current beta is free. We have not launched paid features.

Does NeuraMove automatically import my earnings?

No platform account connection is live. You can log shifts and use supported file imports; always review imported records.

How accurate are the tax calculations?

NeuraMove's tax summary is an estimate based on your recorded income, expenses, and mileage. For self-employed drivers, vehicle expense claims generally use eligible actual costs and the business-use share of total kilometres, not the CRA's per-kilometre employee allowance. Keep receipts and a mileage log, and review your situation with a tax professional before filing.

How can I manage my data?

The app currently offers an export of shift and expense records. Contact support for a broader data request or deletion while account controls are being completed.

Is there a mobile app?

An iOS beta is in testing. Android availability is still being worked on.
